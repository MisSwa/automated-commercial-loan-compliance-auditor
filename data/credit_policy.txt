SECTION 1: DEBT SERVICE COVERAGE RATIO (DSCR)
================================================================================

1.1 Standard Requirement
All commercial real estate loans must demonstrate a minimum Debt Service Coverage
Ratio (DSCR) of 1.25x, calculated as Net Operating Income (NOI) divided by total
annual debt service on all proposed and existing obligations combined.

1.2 Property-Type Adjustments
The following minimum DSCR thresholds apply by collateral type:
  - Multifamily residential (5+ units):  1.20x minimum
  - Office and industrial / warehouse:   1.25x minimum
  - Retail and mixed-use:                1.30x minimum
  - Hospitality (hotel / motel):         1.40x minimum
  - Special-purpose properties:          1.40x minimum

1.3 Exception Policy
Loans with a combined DSCR between 1.15x and 1.24x may be considered only when:
  a) The borrower presents strong compensating factors (e.g., LTV <= 60%,
     guarantor net worth >= 2x loan amount, or confirmed signed lease escalations)
  b) Written approval from the Credit Committee is obtained prior to commitment
  c) Enhanced monitoring covenants are included in the loan agreement

Loans with a combined DSCR below 1.15x are ineligible for approval under any
circumstances and must not proceed to underwriting.

1.4 DSCR Calculation
NOI must be based on a trailing-12-month average, verified against tax returns and
financial statements. Projected or forward-looking NOI may supplement but may not
replace historical NOI in the DSCR calculation.

SECTION 2: LOAN-TO-VALUE (LTV) REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

2.1 Standard LTV Limits
The following maximum LTV ratios apply by collateral type:
  - Commercial real estate, owner-occupied:  80%
  - Commercial real estate, investment:      75%
  - Multifamily (5+ units):                  80%
  - Retail:                                  70%
  - Hospitality / hotel:                     65%
  - Industrial and warehouse:                75%
  - Land (unimproved):                       50%
  - Construction loans:                      60% of as-completed value

2.2 Appraisal Requirements
An independent MAI-certified appraisal is required for all commercial loans. The
appraisal must be dated within 12 months of the loan closing date. For loans over
$5,000,000, a second review appraisal by an independent MAI-certified appraiser
is required. All appraisals must comply with USPAP standards.

2.3 LTV Calculation
LTV is calculated as (loan amount) divided by (the lesser of appraised value or
purchase price). For refinances, the lower of current appraised value or original
purchase price is used unless significant capital improvements are documented.

SECTION 3: REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION CHECKLIST
================================================================================

All of the following documents must be present and verified before underwriting
may commence. Incomplete packages will be returned to the loan officer.

3.1 Business Financial Documents
  - Last 3 years of business tax returns (signed and dated)
  - Last 3 years of business financial statements (income statement and balance sheet)
  - Current year-to-date profit and loss statement (dated within 90 days of application)
  - Current year-to-date balance sheet (dated within 90 days of application)

3.2 Borrower and Guarantor Personal Documents
  - Last 2 years of personal tax returns for all principals with 20% or greater ownership
  - Personal financial statement (dated within 90 days) for all principals with 20%+ ownership
  - Government-issued photo identification for all principals with 20%+ ownership
  - Personal credit authorization signed by all guarantors

3.3 Property and Collateral Documents
  - Independent MAI-certified property appraisal (compliant with USPAP)
  - Phase I Environmental Site Assessment (ESA) per ASTM E1527-21 standards
  - Current rent roll (required if any portion of property is income-producing, dated within 30 days)
  - Executed purchase agreement or signed letter of intent (for acquisitions)
  - Property insurance certificate or binder showing bank as additional insured

3.4 Entity and Legal Documents
  - Articles of incorporation or articles of organization
  - Operating agreement or corporate bylaws
  - Certificate of good standing from state of formation (dated within 90 days)
  - Certificate of incumbency or board/member resolution authorizing the loan
  - Organizational chart showing all entities and ownership percentages

3.5 Missing Documents
Any missing documents require a written explanation from the loan officer. A
conditional review may be initiated only if the missing item is on order with a
firm expected delivery date confirmed in writing. Final approval may not be issued
until all required documents are received and verified.

SECTION 4: BORROWER CREDITWORTHINESS
================================================================================

4.1 Personal Credit Score Requirements
  - Minimum acceptable FICO score: 680 for all principals with 20%+ ownership interest
  - Preferred score: 720 or above for standard approval
  - Scores below 680 require credit committee exception approval and strong compensating factors
  - Scores are pulled from all three bureaus; the middle score is used for underwriting

4.2 Business Operating History
  - Minimum 2 years of continuous operation under current ownership or management
  - Start-up businesses (operating less than 2 years) are not eligible under this policy
  - Change of ownership within 24 months of application requires explanation and financial history
    from the predecessor entity where available

4.3 Net Worth and Liquidity Requirements
  - Guarantor(s) combined net worth must equal at least 1.0x the requested loan amount
  - Post-closing liquidity: minimum 10% of the loan amount in verifiable liquid assets
  - Acceptable liquid assets: checking, savings, money market accounts, publicly traded securities
  - Retirement accounts (IRA, 401k), restricted stock, and illiquid real estate equity
    do not qualify toward the liquidity requirement

4.4 Existing Debt Obligations
  - Global debt service coverage (including all existing business and personal debt) must
    meet the applicable DSCR minimum
  - Any delinquency on existing credit obligations within the past 24 months is disqualifying
    unless adequately explained with documented resolution

SECTION 5: RESTRICTED INDUSTRIES AND SECTORS
================================================================================

5.1 Prohibited Sectors — Ineligible for Any Financing
The bank will not extend commercial financing, under any circumstances, to:
  - Cannabis, marijuana, or hemp-related businesses (including ancillary services)
  - Adult entertainment, sexually explicit content, or escort services
  - Firearms manufacturers or dealers operating without valid FFL licensing
  - Payday lending, check-cashing, or title loan operations
  - Businesses under active federal or state criminal investigation or indictment
  - Cryptocurrency exchanges or unlicensed money services businesses

5.2 Elevated Risk Sectors — Require Enhanced Due Diligence and Credit Committee Approval
The following sectors may be considered but require additional documentation and
written credit committee approval before a term sheet or commitment is issued:
  - Gasoline stations and auto repair facilities (environmental / UST risk)
  - Dry cleaning or laundry operations (chemical / environmental risk)
  - Restaurants and food-service establishments (high business failure rate)
  - Assisted living, memory care, and nursing facilities (regulatory and liability risk)
  - Childcare and early education centers (licensing and liability risk)

SECTION 6: LOAN SIZE AND TERM LIMITS
================================================================================

6.1 Loan Size
  - Minimum loan amount: $500,000
  - Maximum loan amount per single credit: $25,000,000
  - Loans above $10,000,000 require executive credit committee approval in addition
    to standard underwriting review

6.2 Loan Terms and Amortization
  - Maximum amortization period: 25 years
  - Maximum loan term (before balloon or maturity): 10 years
  - Interest-only periods: not to exceed 24 months at any point during the loan term
  - Balloon payments must be explicitly disclosed in all commitment letters and loan documents

6.3 Prepayment Provisions
  - Standard step-down prepayment penalty applies to all fixed-rate commercial loans
  - Minimum lockout period: 3 years for loans with an original term of 5 years or greater
  - Defeasance may be accepted as an alternative to prepayment penalty with prior approval
